Running the workshop
on the system

A plain-language walkthrough of every screen in the Motors Mitra system — what it's for, and how to use it. No computer background needed.

Multibrand Car Workshop For Owners, Admins & Store Staff v1.0
01

Signing In

Everyone who uses the system has their own login. This keeps a record of who did what — useful if a mistake ever needs to be traced and fixed.

  1. Open the system's web address in any browser — on a computer, tablet, or phone.
  2. Enter your email and password. Tap the eye icon in the password box if you want to check what you typed before submitting.
  3. Press Sign in. You'll land on the Dashboard.
If sign-in fails

A wrong email or password shows "Invalid email or password." A message about not reaching the server means the internet connection or the system itself is down — try again in a minute, or contact your admin.

You don't need to remember to log out — closing the browser is fine. On a shared computer, though, it's good practice to sign out using the icon at the bottom of the sidebar menu.

02

The Dashboard

The first screen after signing in. It answers "what's happening right now" without opening anything else.

Today's Vehicles
How many different vehicles had a job card opened today.
Open Job Cards
Every job in the workshop that isn't finished, billed, or cancelled.
Awaiting Invoice
Work marked Completed but not yet billed — nothing should sit here for long.
Waiting for Parts
Jobs stuck because a part isn't available yet.
Today's Sales
Total value of invoices raised today.
Today's Purchases
Total value of stock bought in today.
Low Stock
Items at or below the minimum quantity you set for them.
Out of Stock
Items at zero — flagged in red.

Tap any card to jump straight to the relevant list, already filtered. Below the cards, the Quick Actions row gets you into a new Job Card or Purchase in one tap.

03

Customers & Vehicles

Admin + Store

Every job starts here. A customer can own more than one vehicle — add the person once, then add each car underneath them.

Adding a new customer

  1. Open Customers in the sidebar, then New customer.
  2. Enter their name and mobile number (required), and address if known.
  3. Save. The system won't let you save a second customer with the same mobile number — it'll tell you if one already exists, so you don't accidentally create a duplicate.

Adding their vehicle

  1. Open that customer's page, then Add vehicle under the Vehicles section.
  2. Enter the registration number, make, and model. Current KM is optional but useful.
  3. The registration number can be typed in any style — hr06 ab 1234 or HR06AB1234 are treated as the exact same vehicle, so there's no need to worry about spacing or capitals.
Finding a vehicle fast

Use the Vehicles page in the sidebar to search any vehicle on file by registration number, make, or model — you don't need to know which customer it belongs to first.

04

Job Cards

Admin + Store

The Job Card is the center of the whole system — it follows one vehicle's visit from drop-off to bill. Everything else (parts used, labour charged, the final invoice) hangs off this one record.

Opening a job card

  1. From Job Cards → New job card, search the vehicle by registration number. (If the vehicle isn't on file yet, add it under Customers first.)
  2. Fill in current KM, the customer's complaint, and the work required.
  3. Save — the system gives it a permanent number like JC-000014 automatically. You never type this yourself.

Vehicle inspection photos

On the job card, tap any of the labelled buttons — Front, Rear, Left, Right, Interior, Dashboard/KM, Engine Bay, Damage — to photograph that part of the vehicle straight from your phone's camera. There's also a button for a 360° walk-around video. These stay attached to the job card permanently, useful if a customer later disputes existing damage.

Adding parts

  1. In the Parts section, search for the item by its code or name.
  2. Select it, enter the quantity used, and add it. The price is filled in automatically from the item's current selling price — you don't set it by hand.
  3. Stock is reduced immediately.
If there isn't enough stock

The system refuses the request and tells you exactly how many are actually available. Stock can never go negative — even if two people try to use the last one at the same moment, only one of them will succeed.

Added the wrong part or quantity by mistake? Remove it with the bin icon — the stock is put straight back.

Adding labour

Enter a description (e.g. "General Service"), the technician's name if you want it on record, quantity, and rate. The amount is calculated for you.

Job status

The dropdown at the top of the job card tracks where the vehicle stands:

StatusMeaning
DraftJust created, work hasn't started.
Vehicle ReceivedVehicle is physically at the workshop.
Work Started / In ProgressBeing worked on.
Waiting for PartsPaused — a part is on order.
CompletedWork is done. This unlocks the invoice button.
InvoicedBilled. Parts and labour are now locked — see below.
ClosedFully wrapped up after invoicing.
CancelledJob was called off.
Why can't I edit an invoiced job card?

Once a job card is billed, its parts and labour are frozen on purpose — so the invoice a customer was handed always matches what's on record, permanently. If something genuinely needs correcting after billing, that's an admin conversation, not a quick edit.

05

Invoices

The bill the customer takes home — generated straight from a finished job card, never typed up separately.

Job card: Completed→ Open the job card→ Enter discount (optional)→ Generate invoice

The invoice pulls in the parts, labour, and totals directly — there's nothing to re-type, so it can never disagree with the job card it came from. Once generated, open it any time from the Invoices page and use Print / Save as PDF, which uses your browser's own print dialog to hand the customer a paper copy or a PDF.

06

Service History

Answers "what has this vehicle had done before?" — useful when a repeat customer calls or walks in.

Search by registration number, customer mobile, or customer name — whichever you have on hand. Every past job card for that vehicle appears with its date, KM, complaint, and final amount, linking straight through to the full job card or invoice.

07

Items & Suppliers

Admin + Store

Every spare part or consumable the workshop stocks lives here, along with the vendors you buy them from.

Adding an item

  1. From Items → New item, fill in name, category, unit of measure (PCS, LTR, SET…), pricing, and minimum stock level.
  2. A photo is optional but recommended — it helps staff confirm they've picked the right part.
  3. Save. An Item Code like ITM-000042 is assigned automatically — this never changes and is never typed by hand.

Each item's page shows a full stock history: every purchase that brought stock in and every job card that took stock out, in order.

Suppliers

A simple list of who you buy from — name, contact, mobile. Add one before recording your first purchase from them.

08

Purchases

Admin + Store

Recording stock coming into the workshop — from a brand-new part to restocking one you already carry.

  1. From Purchases → New purchase, pick the supplier.
  2. Choose Existing item and search for it, or New item to register something you've never stocked before — right there in the same form.
  3. Enter quantity and cost. Save — stock goes up immediately, and every purchase is given a running Serial No. (1, 2, 3…) that's permanent and never re-used.
Worked example

Buy 5 oil filters → Serial No. 1, stock becomes 5. Buy 10 more later → Serial No. 2, stock becomes 15. One serial number per purchase, no matter the quantity.

09

Reports

Admin + Store

Five tabs, each answering a different business question over a date range you choose.

Sales
Invoice totals by day.
Purchases
Spending by day, and by supplier.
Inventory
Every item's stock and its total value right now.
Workshop
How many job cards fall into each status.
Customers
New customers in range, and how many are repeat visitors.

Any table can be pulled out with Export CSV, which opens straight in Excel.

10

Users & Roles

Admin only

Two kinds of login exist today:

RoleCan do
AdminEverything — including creating staff logins, and Settings.
Store UserCustomers, vehicles, job cards, items, purchases, invoices, reports — day-to-day work.

From the Users page, an admin creates a login for each staff member (their own email and password) and can disable one instantly if someone leaves — no need to delete their history, just switch them off.

11

Backup & Export

Admin only

Two different safety nets, both under Settings.

Data Backup

One click downloads a complete copy of every business record on file — customers, vehicles, items, purchases, job cards, invoices, everything. Keep a downloaded copy somewhere other than the workshop computer, updated every so often.

Excel Export

A second, parallel copy of the data that updates itself roughly every minute, organised into tabs — one per Customers, Vehicles, Items, Purchases, Job Cards, Invoices, and more — so anyone comfortable with Excel can browse or analyse it without touching the live system.
